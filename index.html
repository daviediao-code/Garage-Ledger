1Who we are

Garage Ledger: Car Care (the “app”, package com.garageledger.app) is published on Google Play by AstraCore Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”).

For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation, AstraCore Studio is the data controller for the limited processing described in this policy. In practice that processing happens on your device and under your control — we hold no copy of it.

2Data we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. Specifically, the app contains:

  • no user accounts, sign-in or cloud profile;
  • no analytics or usage-tracking SDK;
  • no advertising SDK and no advertising identifier;
  • no crash-reporting or remote-logging SDK;
  • no access to your location, contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, microphone or camera.

Because nothing is collected, we cannot identify you, and there is no profile of you to build, sell or hand over.

3Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage, which other apps cannot read:

WhatDetails
Vehicles Nickname, make, model, year, current mileage, engine, drivetrain and the optional VIN.
Service records Date, mileage, cost, currency, provider, parts replaced and free-text notes.
Fuel records Date, mileage, volume, cost, currency and station.
Reminders Title, due date or due mileage, and the linked vehicle.
Receipt photos Only images you pick. They are re-encoded and saved as private app files.
Preferences Distance unit, currency, date format, theme, notification setting.

This data lives in the app’s sandbox — a Room database, a DataStore preferences file and internal image files. Other apps on your phone cannot read it.

Android auto-backup is switched off. The app sets allowBackup="false", so your records are never copied to Google Drive or transferred to a new phone unless you export a backup file yourself.

4When data leaves your device

Only when you explicitly ask it to:

  • Backup file — you choose the destination with the system file picker. The archive contains your database and receipt photos.
  • PDF or CSV report — you choose where to save it, or which app to send it to.
  • Share sheet — if you share a record or a report, the receiving app gets that content.

Once a file is outside the app, it is governed by wherever you put it — a cloud drive, email, a messaging app. We receive nothing, and we never upload, sync or back up your data to any service of ours.

5Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and is the merchant of record; we never see your name, card number or billing address.

The app only receives the purchase state — whether the item is owned — and stores it locally so Pro features stay unlocked. Purchase history and payment data are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

6Permissions

The app declares four Android permissions. None of them is used to collect data.

PermissionWhy it is needed
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Show service reminders. Optional — the app works fully without it.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Re-schedule reminder checks after the phone restarts.
VIBRATE Vibrate for reminder notifications.
INTERNET Used only by Google Play Billing. The app itself makes no network calls.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or broad file access. Receipt photos are chosen through the Android Photo Picker, which hands over only the single image you select.

7Notifications & reminders

Service reminders are scheduled locally on your device. There is no push server, no Firebase Cloud Messaging token and no remote trigger: the app checks its own database when Android wakes it up. Reminder text stays on your phone, and notifications are shown only if you grant the permission.

8Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9Retention & deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. There is no server-side copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no deletion request to file with us.

To erase everything:

  • uninstall the app — Android deletes its private storage, including photos; or
  • delete individual vehicles, records and reminders inside the app. Deleting a vehicle also removes its records, reminders and receipt images after a confirmation prompt.

Backup files you exported earlier are outside the app and stay wherever you saved them; delete them from that location (file manager, cloud drive, email) if you no longer want them.

10Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA give you rights to access, correct, port, restrict and erase your personal data, and to object to its processing. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, port or erase — and we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

You keep full control of the on-device data at all times: you can view it in the app, export it as a backup or report at any moment, and delete it as described above. If you believe we hold something about you, write to us and we will confirm or explain within 30 days.

11Security

Records are stored in app-private storage protected by the Android application sandbox, and receipt images are re-encoded before being saved. The only network traffic the app generates is Google Play Billing’s own communication with Google.

About backups: exported backup archives are standard ZIP files and are not encrypted. Treat them as sensitive — they contain your full service history and photos. Store them somewhere you trust, and delete old copies you no longer need.

12Third-party services

The only third party involved is Google, through Google Play (distribution and payment) and Google Play Billing (in-app purchase). Google may collect installation, purchase and diagnostic information independently of this app; that processing is governed by Google’s own policy, not this one.

There are no advertising networks, analytics providers, crash reporters, social plug-ins or data brokers integrated into the app.

13Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes on Google Play. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
